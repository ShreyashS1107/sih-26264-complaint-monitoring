Integrated Dashboard for Monitoring and Tracking Institution-Related Complaint Cases Across AICTE Bureaus/Cells

The problem we are solving

Institutions attract many complaints. Handling them one by one hides patterns.

Challenges
  • Large complaint volumes
  • Several people reporting the same underlying issue
  • Similar complaints worded differently
  • Hard to find related records
  • Hard to track status and actions
  • Limited visibility across bureaus or cells

Complaint lifecycle

Two separate lifecycles run side by side: the complaint itself, and the overlap review. Confirming an overlap does not resolve a complaint.

The overlap-detection engine

It compares a complaint with eligible existing ones and combines four signals into a heuristic score.

Signals and weights
SignalWeight
Institution matching0.35
Complaint subject similarity0.40
Temporal relationship0.15
Complainant matching0.10
Technical: Institution uses the AICTE Permanent ID as a strong signal, with name/location fallback. Subject similarity uses tokenization, stemming, cosine similarity and bigram Jaccard similarity. Time uses a 90-day half-life within a configured date window. Complainant matching uses masked identifiers and complainant type. Weighted signals are combined into one score; no other coefficients are stated here.
Classification vs candidate threshold
Low < 0.45MediumHigh ≥ 0.65

Medium: 0.45 ≤ score < 0.65. The default candidate-generation threshold is 0.45, so Medium and High scores become candidates. Low scores do not.

Illustrative only: hypothetical values

Limitations: scores are heuristic signals, not calibrated probabilities. High does not prove duplication; low does not prove independence. Missing or invalid data affects individual signals. Human review is necessary and the engine must not auto-merge.

Manual review and decision integrity

The engine recommends. An authorized reviewer decides.

Engine recommends

“These two complaints may concern the same issue.” Score and evidence are shown.

Reviewer decides

Pending review → confirmed overlap, or dismissed as independent.
